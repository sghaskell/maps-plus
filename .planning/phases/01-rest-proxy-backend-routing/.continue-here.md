<decisions_made>

- **Dropped `script = tile_proxy.py` from `default/restmap.conf`** — per the authoritative restmap.conf.spec on the live Splunk container, the `script` key is for handlers *not* derived from BaseRestHandler. Setting it forced Splunk into the legacy `/opt/splunk/bin/runScript.py` codepath, which does `os.chdir(os.path.dirname(script))`. A bare filename dirname-s to `''`, which causes `FileNotFoundError: ''`. For BaseRestHandler subclasses, `handler = MODULE.CLASS` alone is sufficient — Splunk resolves directly from the app `bin/`. Took 7 retries over two sessions to surface this because GSD plan 01-02's offline grep-based verification never loaded the conf into a real splunkd.
- **Test 6 expectation adjusted** — "sub-10ms cache hit" was unrealistic. Splunk REST framework overhead (TLS, auth, routing) imposes a ~100ms floor. 2.4x speedup on identical payload (269→112 ms) is sufficient evidence.
</decisions_made>
